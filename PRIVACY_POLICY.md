## Summary
*Rollivalry: Marble Race* ("Rollivalry") is a free, offline-first 3D marble racing and local Wi-Fi party game for Android. We do **not** collect, sell, track, or upload personal data to external servers.

## 1. Data Collection & Local Storage
Rollivalry does **not** collect, transmit, rent, or store personal data on remote servers. Your chosen display name, Campaign unlocks, best lap times, and settings are stored strictly locally on your device.

## 2. Zero Ads & Zero Tracking SDKs
Rollivalry contains **zero third-party advertising networks**, **zero behavioral analytics SDKs**, and **zero public online chat**.

## 3. Local Wi-Fi Party Multiplayer (LAN Only)
Local multiplayer operates peer-to-peer between devices on your same home Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot (`NsdManager` mDNS + local TCP/UDP sockets). Network permissions (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE`, `NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES` with `neverForLocation`, and `ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK` on Android 17+) are used exclusively for local room discovery and real-time sync. Game state and lobby names stay inside your local network and are discarded when a match ends.

## 4. Camera Permission (QR Room Joining)
`android.permission.CAMERA` is requested only if you tap **Scan QR** in the Wi-Fi Party lobby to scan a host's local Room Code QR image. Camera frames are decoded strictly on-device in memory and are never stored or transmitted.

## 5. Free Base Game & Optional Future DLC Packs
Rollivalry's base game is free. Optional future Level Packs or Special Mode DLC packs may be offered later via **Google Play Billing**. Any transactions are handled solely by Google Play; Rollivalry never receives or stores payment details.

## 6. Children & Families Privacy
Rollivalry is designed for family-friendly couch and Wi-Fi party play with zero third-party ads, zero tracking, and zero external chat.

## 7. Data Deletion
All saved data lives locally on your device; uninstalling the app removes it immediately.
