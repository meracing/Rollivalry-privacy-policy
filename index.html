Rollivalry: Marble Race · Android Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Application: Rollivalry: Marble Race (com.rollivalry.marblerace)
Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Developer: Rene Cornella (renecornella@gmail.com)
Summary: Rollivalry: Marble Race is a free, offline-first 3D marble racing and local Wi-Fi party game. We do not collect, sell, track, or upload your personal data to external servers.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

Rollivalry does not collect, transmit, rent, or store personal data on remote servers. Your chosen in-game display name, Campaign progression, unlocked marbles and colors, best lap times, and control/audio preferences are saved strictly locally on your device.

2. Zero Ads & Zero Tracking SDKs

Rollivalry contains no third-party advertising networks, no behavioral analytics SDKs, and no user profiling or public online chat.

3. Local Wi-Fi Party Multiplayer (LAN Only)

Multiplayer matches run directly peer-to-peer between devices on the same local Wi-Fi router or personal hotspot using Android Network Service Discovery (NsdManager) and local TCP/UDP sockets. Requested permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES with neverForLocation, and ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK on Android 17+) are used solely for local Wi-Fi room discovery and real-time gameplay sync. Lobby names and race inputs remain inside your local network and are discarded when a match ends.

4. Camera Permission (QR Room Joining)

Camera access (android.permission.CAMERA) is optional and requested only when you tap Scan QR in the Wi-Fi Party lobby to scan a host's local Room Code QR image. Camera frames are decoded strictly on-device in volatile memory and are never saved, recorded, logged, or transmitted.

5. Free Base Game & Optional Future DLC Packs

The core game — including all 40 Campaign courses, 8 Special Modes (51 arenas), and Local Wi-Fi Party Multiplayer — is free. In future updates, optional downloadable content (such as additional Level Packs or Special Mode Packs) may be offered. Any optional purchases would be processed exclusively through Google Play Billing; Rollivalry never receives, accesses, or stores payment card or financial account information.

6. Children & Family Privacy

Rollivalry is designed for family play and contains no third-party ads, no behavioral tracking, and no open internet chat rooms.

7. Data Deletion

Because all game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, uninstalling Rollivalry: Marble Race immediately deletes all locally stored data.

8. Changes & Contact

If this Privacy Policy is updated (for example, when optional Level Pack DLCs are introduced), the revised version and effective date will be posted on this page. Questions can be sent to renecornella@gmail.com.
